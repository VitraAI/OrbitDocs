---
title: Bookings
---

A booking moves through three states:

<Steps>
<Step>**pending**: seats are being held.</Step>
<Step>**confirmed**: seats are yours; a `booking.confirmed` webhook is sent. Choose seats or correct names with Update a booking (`booking.changed`).</Step>
<Step>**cancelled**: seats are released; a `booking.cancelled` webhook is sent.</Step>
</Steps>
